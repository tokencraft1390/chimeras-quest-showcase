# Ownership and Chain of Title

## Public ownership statement

Chimera’s Quest is publicly presented as an original game concept created by Zachary Ribble.

No source code, artwork, music, narrative material, character design, trademark rights, or other intellectual property is transferred by publication of this showcase.

## Controlled chain-of-title policy

Before any public release, investment closing, publishing agreement, or asset sale, the private project record should contain:

- dated concept and design records;
- creator and contributor identities;
- signed contractor or contributor agreements;
- written IP assignments where required;
- invoices and receipts for commissioned work;
- license records for every third-party dependency and asset;
- AI-tool usage records and applicable commercial-use terms;
- release approvals for every public screenshot, video, and build.

## Current evidence boundary

This repository is a public showcase, not the complete chain-of-title archive. Confidential agreements, identity documents, source files, credentials, and unreleased assets remain outside the public repository. Their absence here does not establish that they exist; verification is required during diligence.

## Entity and transaction boundary

No entity ownership, assignment, license, investment right, publishing right, or revenue share should be inferred unless confirmed by a signed written agreement.
