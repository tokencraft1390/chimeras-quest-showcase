# Development Roadmap

This roadmap communicates development gates without disclosing proprietary mechanics, unreleased narrative, source code, or production assets.

## Gate 1 — Rights and scope

- approve the public concept summary and intended audience;
- identify the owning party and document chain of title;
- establish the dependency, asset, and AI-use inventories;
- define the protected information boundary.

## Gate 2 — Controlled prototype

- lock the smallest fundable prototype scope;
- build or package a limited, non-sensitive demonstration;
- complete baseline security and dependency review;
- document build reproducibility and test evidence.

## Gate 3 — Validation

- conduct controlled playtesting;
- record usability, accessibility, stability, and performance findings;
- correct material defects;
- approve screenshots and footage for disclosure.

## Gate 4 — Commercial readiness

- finalize the production budget and milestone schedule;
- complete copyright and trademark review;
- prepare publisher, funder, or licensing diligence materials;
- define platform, distribution, support, and release obligations.

## Gate 5 — Public release decision

Public release occurs only after ownership, provenance, security, privacy, platform compliance, and commercial approvals are documented.

Dates and completion claims will be added only when verified.
