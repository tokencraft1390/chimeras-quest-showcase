# Funding Use and Milestones

Chimera’s Quest is seeking appropriate non-dilutive funding, prototype support, publishing conversations, licensing opportunities, and strategic development partnerships.

## Intended use of funds

Any funding plan will be tied to an approved budget and may support:

- prototype and vertical-slice development;
- original art, animation, music, and sound;
- engineering, quality assurance, accessibility, and security;
- platform compliance and release preparation;
- legal review, rights clearance, and trademark work;
- player testing and evidence-based product validation;
- distribution materials and controlled marketing.

## Funding controls

No funding amount, valuation, revenue forecast, delivery date, player count, or commercial traction is claimed in this repository unless supported by dated evidence.

Before accepting funds, the project will document:

1. the recipient individual or legal entity;
2. the funding instrument and its repayment, equity, IP, exclusivity, and control terms;
3. the milestone schedule and permitted uses;
4. reporting and approval obligations;
5. ownership of work produced with the funds.
